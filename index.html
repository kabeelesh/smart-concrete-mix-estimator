Quantity Surveying Calculation Disclosure

This audit trail reveals every intermediate mathematical formulation, conversion factor, and rounding rule used in this estimation project:

Step 1: Wet to Dry Concrete Volume Factor
Dry Volume (m³) = Wet Compacted Volume (m³) × 1.54
Accounts for 54% loss due to dry aggregate voids being filled by water and paste during compaction.
Step 2: Nominal Volumetric Proportion Parts
Sum of Parts (Σ) = c + s + a
For M20 (1 : 1.5 : 3): Σ = 1 + 1.5 + 3 = 5.5 parts total.
Step 3: Wastage Factor Multiplier
Wastage Multiplier = 1 + (Wastage% / 100)
Applied directly to constituent ingredient volumes before packaging calculations.
Step 4: Cement Commercial Bag Rounding Rule
Cement Bags = Math.ceil( Cement Volume (m³) / 0.0347 )
Standard 50 kg OPC bag = 0.0347 m³ (Density = 1440 kg/m³). In commercial civil practice, partial bags cannot be ordered, so volume is rounded UP to next integer bag.
Step 5: Water Requirement & Water-Cement Ratio
Water (Litres) = Total Cement Mass (kg) × 0.50 (w/c ratio)
Assuming standard nominal mix ratio of 0.50 for ordinary workable concrete.
Step 6: Imperial & Secondary Site Units
1 m³ = 35.3147 cu ft | 1 Brass = 100 cu ft = 2.8317 m³ | 1 m³ Sand ≈ 1.6 T
Provided to assist field site supervisors and procurement officers in regional Indian markets.
